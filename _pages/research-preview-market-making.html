Reservation-price shifts

Long inventory moves the reservation price lower; short inventory moves it higher. Top-of-book imbalance can shift both quotes toward the heavier side, while a volatility coefficient widens the base spread during fast moves.

Quote gates

Quoting can pause when short-term movement breaches a volatility threshold. It also stops when the loss limit is breached, when proposed exposure exceeds the notional limit or during the configured post-fill cooldown.

Microstructure state

What the dashboard measures

  • Mid-price and top-of-book microprice.
  • Displayed spread and cumulative depth by side.
  • Visible queue ahead at each paper quote.
  • Trade-flow direction and top-of-book imbalance.
  • Realized, unrealized and total paper P&L.

Execution approximation

A fill requires more than touching the price

Crossing trade

An aggressive sell must trade at or below the paper bid; an aggressive buy must trade at or above the paper ask.

Top-of-book test

By default, a quote must still be realistically fillable relative to the current best price.

Queue consumption

Visible resting quantity ahead is consumed before any incoming trade quantity can reach the paper quote.

Participation cap

Only a configurable share of the residual trade quantity can fill, followed by a cooldown and quote refresh.

Post-trade diagnostics

Markouts connect fills to adverse selection

Every paper fill records side, quote price, quantity, market spread and mid-price at execution. Signed markouts at 1s, 5s and 10s then measure whether the market moved for or against the passive fill. Queue-ahead state is retained for fill-probability diagnostics.

Research limitation

Paper fills remain a model

L2 public data cannot reconstruct exact queue priority, hidden liquidity, exchange matching details or network latency. The participation cap and queue model make fills less optimistic, but simulated P&L is not a claim of executable return. No live order is ever sent.

Engineering scope

Modular by construction

Dedicated modules cover exchange WebSocket/REST clients, local book state, symbol precision, backoff and reconnection, strategy logic, gating, queue position, portfolio accounting, risk, spread tracking, liquidity diagnostics, markouts and two user interfaces. The repository also includes focused tests for market data, order books, trading, analytics and exchange utilities.

Deployment

Two operating modes

The Streamlit application exposes live charts, parameter controls and CSV exports. The console version provides a lower-overhead view of the same book, quote, fill, queue and P&L state.
