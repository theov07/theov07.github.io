RESEARCH PROGRESSION

Every round changed the market—and the process.

The framework supported reproducible comparisons while the research expanded from quoting rules to volatility structure, flow identities and large-universe relative value.

R0–R1 · Reliable base layer

Common backtester, submission path, inventory-aware and signal-aware quoting variants.

R2 · Explicit payoff landscapes

Turn qualitative manual prompts into game-theoretic scoring and robust allocation decisions.

R3 · Options and volatility

Smile diagnostics, Greeks, passive quoting, hedging and portfolio-level strategy comparisons.

R4 · Counterparty-aware flow

Trader-specific features and selective fading, with toxic components disabled or downweighted.

R5 · Fifty-product universe

Group structure, pair relationships, carry and specialist overlays merged after attribution.

EMPIRICAL RECORD

Paths, diagnostics and outcomes.

All values are competition or backtest units—not investment returns. The final standing remains visible alongside the strong early-round result.

INFRASTRUCTURE AS RESEARCH

The framework was part of the alpha process.

Shared execution, diagnostics, product attribution, ablations and an exact submission archive reduced comparison noise and made failed candidates useful.

LIMIT

A competition is a synthetic market.

In-game liquidity, counterparties and scoring rules do not establish real-market profitability. The value lies in research iteration, integration and disciplined evidence.
