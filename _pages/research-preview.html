Editorial rule

Evidence before performance language

Competition PnL is labelled as in-game or backtest PnL. Market-making fills are explicitly simulated. Forecast improvements are shown against their stated benchmark and include negative results. Lead–lag estimates are treated as statistical price-discovery evidence, not as executable arbitrage.

Research scope

Results with explicit boundaries

The studies distinguish statistical evidence, simulated execution, competition outcomes and real-market performance. Assumptions and interpretation limits are stated alongside the results.
