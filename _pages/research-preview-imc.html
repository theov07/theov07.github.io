Research cycle6 rounds

Round 1

A strong result built on a reliable base layer

The first round combined a common backtester and submission path with increasingly signal-aware quoting. The archived result was 107,674 in-game PnL, rank 77 globally and rank 1 in France.

Research progression

Each round changed the market—and the research process

Manual market design

Model the scoring rule before choosing the action

The manual component was treated as a small mechanism-design problem: reconstruct the payoff mapping, estimate where the field concentrates and select a robust region rather than the sample maximum alone.

Round 3 · options

From quoting rules to volatility structure

The option round forced a portfolio view: implied-volatility diagnostics, exposure by strike, hedging cost and the interaction between passive market making and directional filters.

Round 4

Counterparty-aware, not counterparty-dependent

Official log analysis was used to identify participant-specific flow and test selective fading. Order-book imbalance was an additional input, not a substitute for risk control. When evidence deteriorated, products or modules were disabled instead of being kept alive to preserve a narrative.

Round 5 · portfolio scale

Fifty products required structure, not fifty separate guesses

Product families were screened for internal cohesion, pair correlation and divergence. The final candidate combined specialized pair/carry overlays with distinct directional and cross-group research streams.

Integrated candidate

The strongest archived hybrid reached 1,038,132 in-game PnL over the three-day Round 5 backtest. This is a competition backtest result under the provided simulator and should not be interpreted as real-market performance.

What the result demonstrates

Fast iteration with reviewable evidence

The strongest signal is not one backtest number. It is the ability to move from simple quoting to options, participant-aware flow and large-universe relative value while retaining common tests, diagnostics and integration discipline.

Interpretation limits

A competition is a synthetic market

All PnL values on this page are competition or backtest units. The provided simulator, fictional products, participant behaviour and execution rules differ from live markets. Rankings are factual competition outcomes; strategy profitability is not generalized beyond that environment.

Public result

Outcome preserved with its context

The repository retains the official scorecards alongside strategy code and backtests. This separates factual leaderboard outcomes from simulated candidate PnL and makes the final result independently reviewable.
