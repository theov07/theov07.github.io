EDITORIAL RULE

Outcomes keep their context.

Competition P&L remains labelled as in-game or backtest P&L. Market-making fills remain simulated. Forecasts keep their benchmark. Lead–lag remains statistical price-discovery evidence, not executable arbitrage.

RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Negative results stay visible.

A serious portfolio does not hide unstable specifications, short evaluation samples, execution assumptions or strategies that lost against their benchmark.
